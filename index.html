Final marks = ICA (out of 50) + TEE (out of 100, scaled to 50). Pass needs 40% in the TEE and 40% aggregate; "ICA only" subjects pass at 40% of ICA. Fail either and it counts as F (0 points) until the re-exam. SGPA / CGPA are credit-weighted; 0-credit subjects show but don't count. Entered only the total /100? Then pass assumes TEE ≥ 40%, which the SRB checks separately - marked with *. The SRB gives no CGPA-to-percentage conversion.
