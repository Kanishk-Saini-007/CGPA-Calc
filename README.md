# CGPA Calc

Unofficial SGPA and CGPA calculator for NMIMS MPSTME Shirpur. Every subject and credit loads straight from the Student Resource Book A.Y. 2026-27 - after results, you only enter your marks.

**Live:** https://kanishk-saini-007.github.io/CGPA-Calc/

## What it does

- Covers B Tech (Batch 2026-30) Computer Engineering, Computer Science, IT and AI&ML, plus MBA Tech Computer Engineering (Batch 2026-31). Every semester, every subject, official credits.
- Enter ICA /50 and TEE /100 per subject, or just the total /100. Final marks, grade, SGPA per semester and credit-weighted CGPA update as you type.
- Applies the SRB rules: pass needs 40% in TEE and 40% aggregate, "Only ICA" subjects, no grace marks. Results only - no target planner, no percentage conversion.
- Backup / restore as JSON. Everything stays in your browser (localStorage). No login, nothing is uploaded.

## Notes

- Unofficial. Always verify against your official grade sheet.
- Covers only the programmes and batches documented in the MPSTME Shirpur SRB A.Y. 2026-27. Senior batches follow their own SRB.
- The old link (CGPAPlanner) redirects here.

Built as a learning project.
